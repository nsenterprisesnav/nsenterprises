Kitchen appliance repair from Begumpet, Hyderabad

Ns Enterprises repairs and services kitchen appliances at customers’ homes in Hyderabad and Secunderabad. We are based in Paigah Housing Colony, Viman Nagar, Begumpet, and listed on Google Maps as a chimney service.

Who we are

Ns Enterprises brings over 20 years of experience to kitchen appliance repair in Hyderabad. Our work centres on kitchen chimneys, with hobs, gas stoves, microwave ovens, induction stoves, dishwashers, cooking ranges and water purifiers alongside.

What our experience covers

Many kitchen appliance faults look alike from the outside. A chimney with weak suction may need cleaning, a capacitor or a motor. An induction stove with an error code may need a different pan, a sensor or PCB repair. We check the simple causes first and explain what we find before suggesting any part.

  • Chimney repair, deep cleaning, installation and uninstallation
  • PCB repair and touch panel checks on chimneys, microwaves, induction stoves and dishwashers
  • Hob and gas stove burner, ignition and valve checks
  • Microwave oven, dishwasher and cooking range fault finding

What you can expect

  • A ₹350 visit charge, told to you before booking.
  • A quote for parts and labour after inspection.
  • No repair without your approval.
  • An honest answer if your model or part cannot be supported.

An independent service

We are not an authorised service centre of any brand. If your appliance is under warranty, contact the manufacturer first, as outside repair can affect it.
