How to keep your kitchen chimney cleaner

Regular care can help a kitchen chimney work more effectively and make it easier to spot issues early. Always follow the manufacturer's manual for your specific model.

1. Clean accessible filters regularly

Turn the appliance off and allow it to cool. Remove only the filters the manual says are user-removable, then clean them using the manufacturer's recommended method.

2. Wipe the exterior

Use a soft cloth and a suitable cleaner. Avoid spraying liquid directly into electrical controls, lights or motor openings.

3. Watch for changes in performance

Persistent low suction, abnormal vibration, burning smells or repeated shutdowns can indicate a fault that needs professional inspection.

Safety first: switch off power before routine cleaning. Do not open the motor housing or attempt electrical repairs yourself.

When to request a service visit

If cleaning the user-accessible parts does not help, contact a qualified technician. Ask for the inspection fee and estimate before approving work.
