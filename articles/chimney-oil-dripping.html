Why is my kitchen chimney dripping oil?

Oil on the stove or along the edge of the hood means grease is no longer being held or drained where it should be.

1. Saturated filters

Baffle and mesh filters hold only so much oil. When the openings fill up, droplets run along the filter and fall. Wash user-removable filters as your manual advises.

2. Full oil collector

Auto-clean and filterless models drain oil into a cup or tray. Empty it regularly, with the chimney off and cool.

3. Grease inside the duct

A long, bent or wrongly sloped duct lets oil settle and flow back to the hood.

4. Grease on the blower

Oil thrown from a coated blower wheel lands inside the housing and drips. This needs deep cleaning by a technician.

What you can safely do

Switch off at the socket, let the chimney cool, wash removable filters, empty the collector and wipe the hood. Do not open the motor housing or spray cleaner into it.

Grease near a flame is a fire risk. Do not cook under a chimney that is dripping heavily.
