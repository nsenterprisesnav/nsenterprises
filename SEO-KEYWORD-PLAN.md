# SEO keyword and page plan

Use natural language and only target services the business truly provides.

| Page | Primary search intent | Example topic |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Local appliance repair | appliance repair in Hyderabad |
| Chimney service | Appliance-specific repair | kitchen chimney repair Hyderabad |
| Microwave service | Appliance-specific repair | microwave oven repair Hyderabad |
| Hob service | Appliance-specific repair | gas stove and hob repair Hyderabad |
| Induction service | Appliance-specific repair | induction stove repair Hyderabad |
| Dishwasher service | Appliance-specific repair | dishwasher repair Hyderabad |
| Chimney-care article | Helpful informational content | how to clean a kitchen chimney |
| Microwave-safety article | Helpful informational content | microwave sparking safety |

Do not repeat keywords unnaturally, create thin pages for every neighbourhood, or claim service in an area without actually serving it. Add location pages only when each page offers genuinely useful, unique local information.
