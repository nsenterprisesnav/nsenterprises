COMMON PROBLEMS

Hob & Gas Stove problems we help with

Safer, more reliable cooking starts with a proper inspection.

Auto ignition not working

Often a wet or dirty electrode, weak battery, faulty igniter unit or damaged wiring. The spark is tested at each burner.

Weak or uneven flame

Blocked burner holes, a clogged jet or a supply pressure problem can reduce the flame. The burner set, jet and knob are checked.

Yellow or sooty flame

A yellow flame that blackens vessels means gas and air are not mixing correctly. Keep the kitchen ventilated and have it checked.

Flame goes off by itself

On hobs with a flame-failure device, a dirty or failed thermocouple can shut the burner off after you release the knob.

Knobs stuck or hard to turn

Dried grease or a worn gas valve makes knobs stiff. Do not force them; the valve may need attention.

Burner noise or clogged holes

Popping, whistling or patchy flames usually come from food residue, moisture or a misplaced burner cap.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Hob & Gas Stove Repair FAQs

Why is my hob auto ignition clicking but not lighting?

Moisture or food around the electrode, a blocked burner or low gas flow are common causes. Dry and clean the area when cool; if it still fails, book an inspection.

Why is my gas stove flame yellow?

The burner is not getting enough air or is partly blocked. It wastes gas and leaves soot, so have the burner and jet checked.

How much does gas stove repair cost?

The visit charge is ₹350, and you receive a quote for parts and labour before approving the repair.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Do not switch anything electrical on or off and do not light a flame. Turn off the cylinder regulator if it is safe, open doors and windows, leave the area and call the LPG emergency helpline 1906 or your gas supplier.

Where can I get hob repair near me?

We list kitchen hob service in Hyderabad and Secunderabad, including built in gas stove repair in Malkajgiri and Begumpet. Call your locality to confirm.

Is it safe to repair a gas hob at home?

Cleaning a cool burner cap is fine. Valves, jets, pipes and leaks should be handled only by a trained technician with the gas supply closed.
