Frequently asked questions

How much does a repair cost?

The cost depends on the fault, parts and labour. The visit charge is ₹350. We provide a quote before repair.

Can I book a home visit?

Use the request form or call/WhatsApp to confirm your area and available appointment times.

What details should I share?

Share the appliance brand, model if available, symptoms, your locality and a convenient time to contact you.

What if the appliance seems unsafe?

Stop using it. For gas smell, turn off the gas supply if safe to do so, ventilate the area, avoid switches/flames, leave the area and contact emergency/gas services. Do not attempt internal electrical or gas repairs yourself.
