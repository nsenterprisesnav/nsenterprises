Chimney Installation FAQs

How much does chimney installation cost in Hyderabad?

It depends on the chimney type, duct length and wall work. Call 94939 84204 or send photos on WhatsApp for a quote.

Is the duct pipe included?

Duct pipe, clamps and wall cutting may be extra. Ask for an itemised quote before you approve the work.

Do you do chimney uninstallation only?

Yes, you can book uninstallation alone, for example before moving house or renovating.

Can a filterless chimney work without a duct?

Many chimneys need a duct to the outside. Recirculating models use carbon filters instead; check your model manual.
