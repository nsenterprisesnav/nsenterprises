COMMON PROBLEMS

What can we help with?

Describe what you are seeing so the team can advise on the next step.

Low or no water flow

Share when the flow changed and whether any indicator lights are on.

Leak around the unit

Note where you see water and whether the leak is continuous or occasional.

Filter or service alert

Share the alert shown and your purifier make and model if available.

Unusual taste or odour

Describe the change and when it started. Do not drink water you suspect may be unsafe.

Power or control issue

Tell us whether the purifier turns on and what the display or indicator shows.

Unusual noise

Describe the sound and whether it happens during dispensing or at other times.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently asked questions

What should I share when I book?

Share your purifier make and model if available, the symptoms, any display message and your locality.

Can I request a home visit?

Use the request form or call/WhatsApp to confirm your area and an available appointment time.

How much does service cost?

Charges depend on the issue and any parts or filters needed. Confirm the inspection fee and ask for an estimate before approving work.

What if I think the water is unsafe?

Use a safe alternative source while you arrange help. Do not open electrical parts or pressurized lines yourself.
