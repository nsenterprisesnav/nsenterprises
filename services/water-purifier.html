COMMON PROBLEMS

Water Purifier problems we help with

Common water purifier problems and likely causes.

Low or slow water flow

Choked sediment or carbon filters, low inlet pressure or an ageing membrane can slow the purifier down.

Bad taste or smell

Exhausted carbon filters or an unclean tank are common causes. Stop drinking the water until it has been checked.

Water leakage

Tube fittings, filter housings, the tank and the float are checked to find the source of the leak.

Purifier not turning on

The adapter, power supply, float switch or low-pressure switch may be at fault.

Continuous reject water

On RO purifiers, a faulty solenoid valve, flow restrictor or tank float can keep water draining.

Filter change or service alert

Tell us the model and the light or alarm shown so we can understand which filter it refers to.

Water Purifier Service FAQs

How often should water purifier filters be changed?

It depends on your water and usage. Follow your model manual and have it checked earlier if flow drops or the taste changes.

Why is my water purifier slow?

Blocked pre-filters, low inlet pressure or an ageing RO membrane are the usual causes.

Why does my RO keep draining water?

Some reject water is normal while purifying. If it drains constantly, the solenoid valve, flow restrictor or float may be faulty.
