COMMON PROBLEMS

Dishwasher problems we help with

Common dishwasher faults and what is inspected.

Dishwasher not draining

Food in the filter, a bent or blocked drain hose or a failed drain pump leaves water at the bottom. Each is checked in turn.

Dishes not getting clean

Blocked spray arms, a dirty filter, hard water scale or low water temperature reduce wash quality.

Water leakage

The door seal, hoses, spray arms, sump and water level are checked to find the source of a leak.

Not filling with water

A closed tap, blocked inlet filter, faulty inlet valve or triggered anti-flood float can stop filling.

Cycle stops or shows an error

Note the error code and when it stops. It helps narrow the fault to heating, draining, filling or the control board.

Power, door or control faults

The machine will not start if the door latch does not register. The latch, wiring and control panel are tested.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Dishwasher Repair FAQs

Why is my dishwasher not draining?

Start with the filter and check that the drain hose is not bent. If water remains, the drain pump or hose may be blocked or faulty and needs inspection.

Why are my dishes still dirty after a wash?

Blocked spray arms, overloading, hard water or low salt and detergent are common reasons. If cleaning those does not help, book a visit.

What should I do if my dishwasher leaks?

Stop the cycle, switch off at the socket if it is dry and safe, close the water tap and book an inspection.

How much does dishwasher repair cost in Hyderabad?

The visit charge is ₹350. You approve the parts and labour quote before repair.

What do Bosch dishwasher codes E15 and E24 mean?

E15 usually means the leak protection has been triggered, and E24 usually points to a drainage problem. Close the tap for E15 and check the filter and hose for E24 before booking.
