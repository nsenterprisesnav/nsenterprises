COMMON PROBLEMS

When to book chimney cleaning

Signs that cleaning, not repair, may be what you need.

Oil dripping onto the stove

Saturated filters or a full oil collector can no longer hold grease.

Suction has become weak slowly

Grease narrows the filter openings and coats the blower wheel.

Sticky hood and filters

Hardened oil that normal wiping does not remove needs a degreasing wash.

Burnt-oil smell while cooking

Old grease inside the hood warms up and smells each time you cook.

Chimney louder than before

Uneven grease on the blower wheel can make it vibrate.

Auto-clean no longer enough

Heat auto-clean reduces build-up but does not clean every internal part.

Chimney Deep Cleaning FAQs

What is included in chimney deep cleaning?

It can include dismantling accessible parts, a chemical wash of the filters, blower and oil collector, wiping the hood and a suction check after refitting. Confirm the exact scope and price before work starts.

What is the difference between basic cleaning and deep cleaning?

Basic cleaning covers the outer hood, filters and oil collector. Deep cleaning also reaches the blower and internal surfaces.

How much does chimney cleaning cost in Hyderabad?

It depends on the model, grease level and scope. The visit charge is ₹350; call 94939 84204 for a quote.

How long should I wait between deep cleanings?

Heavy frying needs more frequent cleaning. Go by suction, dripping and smell rather than a fixed date.

Is the chemical wash safe?

Degreasing agents must be kept off electrical parts and rinsed fully. Keep children and pets away and the kitchen ventilated during the work.
